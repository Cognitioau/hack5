ZOMBIE HAND / EXPERIMENTAL

Gaze lab

Try a local camera calibration. This lab does not arm or capture content on other pages.

Camera images and calibration stay in memory on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is saved unless you choose Download session diagnostics. Hiding or closing this page stops the camera and clears the session.

Camera is off. Start only when you are ready.

Nine calibration points are followed by five separate checks. Results describe this session only; they do not establish capture accuracy.

Keep the window, your seat and lighting steady. Look at the small dot in the centre of each target until it shrinks away, then click the target or press Space. Moving or resizing the window requires recalibration.

After the checks, Download session diagnostics saves one JSON file to your downloads, and only when you click it. It contains the window size and position, the target positions, the ten geometric eye measurements computed for each processed camera frame during calibration and checks (iris offsets within each eye, the eye-centre position and inter-eye distance in the camera image, and head-turn ratios), the chosen model and its error table, each check’s error, and summary statistics of how much the live dot moved. It contains no camera images, video frames or face-mesh landmark arrays. Nothing is uploaded.
