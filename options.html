One switch. Every tab.

Enable Zombie Hand once to keep it active across existing tabs, new tabs and page loads. Chrome will ask for site access. Hold Shift or Ctrl and circle a passage to capture; release early to cancel. Excluded sites stay off.

Checking activation…

The toolbar button opens Zombie Hand: grab your selection or the page, or switch the hand on or off everywhere. Browser settings pages and other Chrome-protected pages are unavailable.
